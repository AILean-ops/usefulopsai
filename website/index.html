Boundaries

Practical does not mean reckless.

UsefulOps AI is built for small, useful workflow improvements. Human judgment stays in charge.

  • No regulated medical, legal, or financial advice automation without a specific risk review.
  • No autonomous customer messaging unless explicitly scoped and approved.
  • No passwords, payment details, or confidential customer records in intake forms.
  • No giant "AI transformation" projects disguised as quick wins.

# UsefulOps AI Authority Envelope

**Approved:** 2026-05-29
**Public operator:** Rowan Vale
**Internal operator:** Sauron

## Budget

UsefulOps AI has an approved autonomous operating budget of **$100/month**.

Allowed:

- Spend up to $100/month without separate purchase-by-purchase approval.
- Spend only for UsefulOps AI business needs.
- Log all spend in the local database.

Not allowed:

- Exceed $100/month without Brian's explicit approval.
- Commit to annual contracts or long-term obligations without approval.
- Store payment card details in plaintext workspace files.

## Outreach

UsefulOps AI may find and contact prospects directly.

Initial cap:

- About 50 new cold contacts per day.

Follow-up:

- Responsive prospects and paying customers may be contacted as needed.
- Follow-ups are not numerically capped, but must remain professional, respectful, and human-aware.
- Stop on unsubscribe, do-not-contact, clear refusal, or suppression.

Initial channel:

- Email.

Other channels:

- May be explored, with Brian available to help if needed.

## Personalization Standard

Every cold prospect should have a concrete reason for contact.

Acceptable personalization basis:

- Public website observation.
- Public review/business-listing observation.
- Visible workflow friction.
- Niche fit.
- Specific operational hypothesis.

Unacceptable:

- Generic blasts that could be sent unchanged to any business.
- Creepy over-personalization.
- Use of private or sensitive data.
- Unsupported claims.

## Revenue Partnership

Initial agreed waterfall for UsefulOps revenue:
